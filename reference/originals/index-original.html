All You Need to Know

How does SkillSync calculate opportunity match scores?

SkillSync uses vector embeddings based on your self-reported skill proficiencies, university background, and project history. Our autonomous agent compares these against extracted event requirements in real-time.

Is my profile data private?

Yes! You have full control over your privacy mode. You can set your profile to Public, Team Formation Only, or completely Hidden via the My Profile tab.

Can I invite non-SkillSync students to my team?

Absolutely. You can generate custom team invite links to share across Discord, Slack, or email.
