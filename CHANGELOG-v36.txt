SkillSync v36 — tier ranking and filter

- Tier 2: one or multiple profile skill/interest overlaps.
- Tier 4: listed learning/career opportunity with no profile overlap.
- Tier 5: unrelated email.
- Added a Match Tier dropdown filter to the opportunity sidebar; existing percentage score filter remains available.
