About SkillSync

Practical learning opportunities like hackathons, competitions, and workshops are fragmented across platforms, creating search fatigue and causing students to miss skill-building and networking events. SkillSync is an agentic AI platform designed to automate the discovery, curation, and personalization of practical learning experiences. SkillSync delivers ranked recommendations, explicit eligibility checks, and real-time updates for deadline changes. The platform promotes equitable learning access, highlights sustainability-focused opportunities, and enables consent-based peer matching to foster collaborative learning.

All You Need to Know

How does SkillSync calculate opportunity match scores?

SkillSync compares your self-reported skills, university background, and project history against extracted opportunity requirements in real-time.

Is my profile data private?

Yes! You have full control over your privacy mode. You can set your profile to Public, Team Formation Only, or completely Hidden via the My Profile tab.

Can I invite non-SkillSync students to my team?

Absolutely. You can generate custom team invite links to share across Discord, Slack, or email.
